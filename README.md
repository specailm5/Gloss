# Gloss

Right-click any word on a page to see what it means — in a small popup, without
leaving the page.

## What you can look up

- **Words** — everyday meanings, with the part of speech.
- **People and places** — a short summary, a photo, and facts such as date of birth,
  country, population, or headquarters.
- **Acronyms and jargon** — technical terms that a normal dictionary doesn't cover.
- **Books, papers, artworks, chemicals, species and code tags** — when a word has
  no dictionary entry, Gloss tries these collections before giving up.
- **Pronunciation** — phonetic spelling, with audio when a recording exists.
- **Other meanings** — when a word has several senses, related articles are one
  click away.

## How to use it

1. Select a word or name on any page.
2. Right-click and choose **What is "…"?**
3. A bubble appears next to your selection.

Close it with the **×** button, the **Esc** key, or by clicking anywhere else.
Clicking a suggested meaning looks that article up in place.

## Install

### Chrome / Edge

1. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`).
2. Turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Choose **Load unpacked** and select this folder.

### Firefox

1. Open `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`.
2. Choose **Load Temporary Add-on…** and select `manifest.json`.

## Privacy

- No account, no sign-up, no analytics.
- The word you look up is sent to the public knowledge sources below; Gloss has no
  server of its own.
- Results are cached on your device so repeat lookups are instant.
- Nothing is downloaded to your computer — there is no AI model or extra data pack.

## Sources

Definitions and facts come from [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org),
[Wiktionary](https://www.wiktionary.org), [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org),
[Datamuse](https://www.datamuse.com) and
[dictionaryapi.dev](https://dictionaryapi.dev). Books come from
[Open Library](https://openlibrary.org), papers from
[Crossref](https://www.crossref.org), artwork from the
[Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu), chemicals from
[PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov), species from
[GBIF](https://www.gbif.org), and code tags from
[Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com). Map links open
[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org).

## Known limits

- Gloss can't show a bubble on browser-internal pages (such as `chrome://` or
  `about:`) or inside the built-in PDF viewer.
- A word with no entry anywhere still gets a **Search the web** link, so it is never
  a dead end.
